Who We Are

SRC Infraprojects Pvt. Ltd. (SRCIPL)

We provide technology-driven surveying, geospatial and land management services to public-sector organisations across Uttar Pradesh and other parts of India.

Our work sits where land records meet ground reality. We integrate revenue records, approved layout plans, physical ground conditions and geospatial survey data, so authorities can make decisions on verified facts.

Our mission

To give public authorities accurate, well-documented land and infrastructure data that supports transparent planning, asset protection and development.

Our Approach

Five steps. No shortcuts between them.

Understand the requirement

Study the scope, records and plans.

Establish control

Ground control points with dual-frequency DGPS/GNSS.

Capture data

Drone, LiDAR or echo sounder, as the site requires.

Process and verify

CAD and GIS processing, cross-checked with records and ground verification.

Deliver

Maps, models, databases and reports in formats ready to use.

Quality & Accuracy

Nothing leaves our hands unchecked.

  • Every survey tied to DGPS/GNSS ground control
  • Independent check points to verify accuracy before delivery
  • Field data cross-checked against records and ground verification
  • Survey and processing records kept for every project
  • Data handled confidentially; client data is never shared or published
