Integrated Land Audit

One verified view of every plot.

Development schemes often carry gaps between what was approved, acquired, possessed and what actually exists on the ground. We combine revenue records, layout plans, DGPS survey, drone imagery, LiDAR, GIS and field verification into a single Digital Land Asset Database.

  • Approved, acquired and possession area
  • Actual physical area on the ground
  • Plot-wise discrepancies and encroachments
  • Vacant land and development status

LiDAR Survey

High-precision reality capture for land, buildings, infrastructure and corridors.

LiDAR captures dense 3D point clouds, even under vegetation and in complex spaces, geo-referenced with RTK/GNSS and ground control points.

  • 3D laser scanning — buildings, industrial facilities, heritage structures, interiors and exteriors
  • Mobile / handheld LiDAR — rapid site scans, indoor mapping, obstructed areas, as-built records
  • Drone LiDAR — large terrain, vegetated and inaccessible areas, corridors
  • LiDAR topography — ground surface, contours, DEM/DTM, breaklines, spot levels, terrain classification
  • Roads & infrastructure — carriageway, drainage, bridges, flyovers, utility corridors, as-built surveys
  • Volume & change analysis — earthwork, stockpiles, cut and fill, construction progress
Deliverables
LAS/LAZPLYClassified point cloudDEM/DTMContour maps3D meshGeoTIFF
