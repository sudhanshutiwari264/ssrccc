We are a technology-driven Survey Consultancy

SRC Infraprojects Pvt. Ltd. (SRCIPL) specialises in land surveying, geospatial technology and land asset management. We combine revenue records, approved layout plans, physical ground conditions and high-precision survey data to give public-sector organisations a clear, verified picture of their land and assets.
