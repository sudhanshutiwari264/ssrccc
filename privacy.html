1. What this policy covers

This Privacy Policy explains what information SRC Infraprojects Pvt. Ltd. ("SRCIPL", "we", "us") collects through this website, why we collect it, how long we keep it, and how you can access or request deletion of it.

2. Information we collect

  • Contact and organisation details submitted through the Get a Quote and Quick Enquiry forms — name, organisation, designation, mobile number, email address, postal address and GSTIN (optional).
  • Project documents uploaded with a quote request — tender/NIT documents, layout plans, revenue maps, BOQs, drawings and related files.
  • Career applications submitted through the Join Us form — name, mobile number, email address, qualification, experience, skills, preferred role and CV.
  • Basic technical data generated automatically when you browse the site, such as browser type and pages visited, for security and site-improvement purposes.

3. Why we collect it

  • To review and respond to quotation requests and enquiries.
  • To evaluate career applications for current or future openings.
  • To prepare technical and commercial proposals for prospective clients.
  • To meet legal, tax and regulatory record-keeping requirements.

4. How long we keep it

We retain quotation, project and application data for as long as reasonably necessary to service the request, maintain project records, and meet legal or contractual obligations, after which it is deleted or anonymised.

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell client or applicant data. Information is shared only with SRCIPL personnel involved in evaluating your request, or where required by law or a government authority.

6. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by writing to DK_SRCGROUP@REDIFFMAIL.COM, subject to applicable law and our legitimate record-keeping requirements.

7. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect submitted information against unauthorised access, alteration or disclosure. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.

9. Contact

For any privacy-related query, write to DK_SRCGROUP@REDIFFMAIL.COM.
